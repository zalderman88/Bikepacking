    Routes and sources

    Road choice. Each leg has a dirt version and a main-road version. Five legs follow a mapped bikepacking.com dirt route (Baja Divide, Trans Ecuador, Peru Great Divide, Lagunas y Salares and the Seis Miles), and auto-pick never moves those onto a highway, though it may suggest a bus. Elsewhere the dirt line links back roads between the towns shown, and the main-road distance is the road distance between the same towns.

    Safety. Two legs cross areas with current government warnings, and auto-pick buses them: Mazatlán to Durango and Zacatecas (US advisory, Oct 2026), and the Bajo Cauca between Cartagena and Medellín (UK advice, Oct 2026). Check both again before you go.

    Baja Divide (bikepacking.com): 2,784 km of sandy dirt from the US border down the peninsula, about 42 days, best Nov to Mar. Route page

    Trans-Mexico (bikepacking.com), the Trans Mexico on your chart. Norte runs Mazatlán to Oaxaca (2,363 km, Dec to Apr) and Sur runs Oaxaca to Frontera Corozal (1,094 km, Nov to Mar); both are about half unpaved. The Mexico lines on the map follow the main towns, so use these for the actual dirt. Norte · Sur

    Dan Camp, Dan Cycles the World: films from one Alaska to Argentina ride, across southern Mexico (Oaxaca to the Guatemala border), Oaxaca backroads, Nicaragua, southern Bolivia, and the Atacama and puna in Chile. S Mexico · Oaxaca · Nicaragua · S Bolivia · Atacama

    Trans Ecuador Mountain Bike Route (bikepacking.com): the dirt road version is used here, 1,381 km to Vilcabamba, mid-June to mid-September. Iohan rode the singletrack version. Dirt road · Singletrack

    Peru Great Divide (bikepacking.com): high dirt route along the Andes from Huaraz to Abancay, 1,605 km, about 32 days, April or May to September. Route page

    Ruta de las Tres Cordilleras (bikepacking.com): Sorata to Pitumarca near Cusco, 793 km, mid-May to early September. The dirt option for Cusco to La Paz. Route page

    Lagunas y Salares (bikepacking.com): the Salar and the Lagunas to Chile, 734 km, May to October. Route page

    Ruta de los Seis Miles (bikepacking.com). Norte: San Pedro de Atacama to Fiambalá over Paso Socompa, 784 km, Nov to Mar. Sur: Fiambalá to Guandacol, 526 km, Oct to Apr, north to south only. Norte · Sur

    Iohan Gueorguiev, The Bike Wanderer: Colombia from December 2016 to May 2017, the Trans Ecuador singletrack, Las Tres Cordilleras and To Titicaca via the Land of Gold in southern Peru, and the Ruta de los Seis Miles. Colombia · Ecuador · Tres Cordilleras · Titicaca · Seis Miles

    Boats and roads. Austral Bikes on the O'Higgins crossing · Panama to Colombia by boat · Ruta 40 paved and gravel · TDA on Patagonia

    Climate. Southern Colombia uses the IDEAM 1991 to 2020 normals for Popayán and Pasto. Seasons elsewhere were checked against 20 years of Open-Meteo reanalysis and national station tables.

    Distances are planning numbers for each leg, not GPX totals: guide figures for the mapped routes, road distances between the towns for the main-road versions, and estimates elsewhere. Climate values are typical monthly averages for a representative spot on each leg, and nights at camp altitude in the Andes can run colder. Season bands follow your chart where it covers a region. Check the latest travel advice, conditions, border rules and boat schedules before you commit. Header artwork made in Canva.
